# Analytics Workflows & User Guides

## 1. Executive Intelligence Dashboard (`/analytics`)
- High-density executive command center using Deep Wine design system.
- Domain tabs: Overview, Organization, Ranks & Quals, Compensation & Ledger, CRM Sales Funnel, Productivity, Community & Leadership.
- Filter by period, scope, or rank. Export CSV reports directly.

## 2. Team Analytics (`/analytics/team`)
- Scoped downline view for organizational leaders based on PostgreSQL recursive CTE downline resolution.

## 3. Member Intelligence Profile Tab (`/network/members/[memberId]`)
- Consolidated intelligence tab on member profile combining rank history, productivity score, engagement score, and CRM lead metrics.
