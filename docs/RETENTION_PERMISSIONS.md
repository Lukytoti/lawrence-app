# Client Retention Permissions & Client Privacy Controls

## 1. System Permissions (13)
- `retention:read_own`: Read own client retention records.
- `retention:read_team`: Read team client retention records.
- `retention:read_all`: Read all client retention records.
- `retention:create`: Create retention records & touchpoints.
- `retention:update`: Update retention records & success plans.
- `retention:assign`: Assign retention cases & renewals.
- `retention:manage_cases`: Manage retention cases.
- `retention:manage_renewals`: Manage contract renewals.
- `retention:manage_success_plans`: Manage success plans.
- `retention:manage_health`: Manage client health scoring.
- `retention:manage_expansion`: Manage expansion opportunities.
- `retention:admin`: Full retention administration.
- `retention:export`: Export retention reports.

## 2. Client Privacy Controls
Client portal access is strictly scoped to the client's own email. Internal risk notes, retention comments, and staff case assignments are never exposed to clients.
