# Strategic Product Roadmap

## Stage 1: Foundation (Current Implementation)
- Core App Shell, Modern Dark-Wine Design System, Navigation Architecture (9 categories).
- NextAuth Authentication & Server-Side Dynamic RBAC.
- Foundational Prisma Database Schema, Migrations, Seeding.
- Global Command Palette (`Cmd+K`).
- Notification Infrastructure & Audit Logging Service.
- Event Bus Architecture (`EventBus`).
- Documentation & Vitest Automated Testing Setup.

## Stage 2: User & Profile Management Module (Future)
- Member onboarding & profile management.
- Custom security roles & permissions assignment UI.
- User status management (`ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `INACTIVE`).

## Stage 3: Network Marketing Organization Engine (Future)
- Unlimited-depth organization tree visualization.
- Effective-dated upline/sponsor history tracking.
- Member movement auditing & success lines.

## Stage 4: Productivity Engine — Tasks, Activities & Reports (Future)
- Member "My Day" workspace.
- Daily/weekly/monthly activity logging & evidence submission.
- Activity verification & report approval workflow.

## Stage 5: Learning, Books & Training Module (Future)
- Book reading assignment & milestone tracking.
- Training tracks, video/document modules, progress reports.

## Stage 6: Digital Marketing, Projects & Outsourcing (Future)
- Client CRM portal.
- Project & milestone management.
- Task outsourcing to organization members.

## Stage 7: Immutable Financial Ledger & Earnings Engine (Future)
- Append-only financial ledger (`LedgerEntry`).
- Compensation & earnings calculation engines.
- Financial payout requests, approvals, disputes, and audit trails.

## Stage 8: Leadership, Coaching & AI Assistant (Future)
- One-on-one coaching logs & target setting.
- AI leadership assistant & automated reminders.
